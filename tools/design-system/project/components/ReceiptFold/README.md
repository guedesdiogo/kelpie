# ReceiptFold

When a conversation or task finishes, its detail folds into a summary in one movement, and the summary lists the real vault paths the work used (5.18, moment grammar).

- **The fold:** height, and a vertical scale to 0.98, with `spring-moment`. It is one of the contained size changes allowed outside `transform` and `opacity`.
- **The summary** lists the real vault paths the work used, as links, and says who did it, when and for how long ("Ran 4 min 12 s").
- **Receipts say who did what and when,** in the owner's time zone (Voice).
- **The consumer provides** the detail, the summary line and the paths.
- **Reduced motion:** a fade.
