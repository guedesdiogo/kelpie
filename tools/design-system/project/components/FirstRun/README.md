# FirstRun

The first run puts comfort first, one step with motion, text size, contrast and the mascot's pause, then welcomes the first agent with a pop and a smile (5.17, moment grammar).

- **Comfort comes first.** Before anything else, one step sets motion (full or less), text size, contrast and the mascot's pause (Alive, States only or Still), with the DisplayMenu's controls: switches for less motion and more contrast, the mascot's outline icons for its pause. Each switch starts with its icon: a circle with one trail for less motion, a square split on its diagonal for more contrast (never the half-filled circle, which means working). Text size is a segmented control of two outlined letters, a smaller and a larger A, each named in its tooltip; that control comes from the §8 research (Lucide's `a-large-small`, Material's `contrast_square` and `motion_blur`), and the owner approved it on 2026-10-16. That is where the less-motion switch of rule 10 lives. The step can be reopened in settings. Until the management UI has them, the webchat's DisplayMenu holds the switch, the theme, the contrast and the mascot's pause.
- **The first agent.**
  - Until it exists, the empty state says in one sentence why the roster is empty, and offers one button.
  - When it is created, its mascot enters its slot with `spring-pop` and plays Idle's hop and smile, and the empty block collapses with `spring-moment`.
- **Channels and secrets** are asked at the step that needs them, not up front.
- **The consumer provides** the stored choices and the create-agent action.
- **Reduced motion:** fades.
